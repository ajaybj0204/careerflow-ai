CareerFlow AI Companion

Create a personal token in CareerFlow → Settings → Chrome extension, then paste it here.

Autofill

Fill button on job sites

Show the “Fill with CareerFlow” button automatically on application pages of Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Keka, Darwinbox, iCIMS, Naukri, Indeed and other job sites. Without this, use the popup button or Alt+Shift+F on any site.

Privacy

  • Autofill uses only facts from your CareerFlow profile and your saved answers. Personal questions (gender, date of birth, caste, ID numbers) are never filled.
  • It never clicks Submit. You review every form and submit it yourself.
  • On job sites you allow, it only checks whether the page has an application form. It does not record your browsing.
  • The extension reads a page only when you click “Capture” or “Fill”, or on job sites you allowed.
  • It does not track your browsing, store portal passwords, or submit applications.
  • It talks only to the CareerFlow URL you enter here.
