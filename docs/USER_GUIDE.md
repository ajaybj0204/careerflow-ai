# CareerFlow AI — user guide

## 1. Set up once
1. **Sign in** and complete onboarding (career stage, target roles, roles to avoid, locations, CTC).
2. **AI Resume Studio → Upload master resume** (DOCX keeps your exact template; PDF works with an editable rebuilt template you approve).
3. **My Career Profile**: confirm the facts extracted from your resume. Edit or remove anything wrong. Only confirmed facts are ever used by AI.
4. **Settings → Connect Gmail** (optional) and import the last 30–90 days.

## 2. Find and save jobs
- **Discover Jobs**: search permitted feeds; each listing shows its source, freshness and why it matches.
- **Job Portal Hub**: open Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, Foundit and others with your keywords.
- **Chrome extension**: on any job page click *Capture this job posting*, check the details, *Save*.

## 3. Prepare and apply
- Open the job's **workspace** → *Job description* → paste the JD → *Save and analyse* for a match report.
- **Tailor resume**: accept, reject or edit AI suggestions (blocked ones explain why), or edit paragraphs yourself. *Create DOCX & PDF*.
- **Cover Letters** and **Application Copilot** for letters and form answers. Save good answers to your library.
- Apply on the original portal, then mark the job **Applied** (or let the Gmail confirmation do it).
- Record which resume you submitted with *I submitted this version*.

## 4. Track
- **Applications**: filters, bulk updates, CSV/Excel export, timeline.
- **Needs Review**: confirm emails CareerFlow was unsure about. Nothing is marked rejected or selected on unclear evidence.
- **Dashboard / Calendar**: today's actions, interviews, assessment deadlines, follow-ups.

## 5. Interviews and growth
- **Interview Studio → Interview Tomorrow**: 15/30/60/120-minute plan + revision PDF.
- **Mock interviews** (text or voice), **Scenario Lab**, **Skills Academy** practice and learning plans.
- After a real interview, **Post-interview review** updates your weakness tracker and question bank.

## 6. Offers
- Upload an offer letter to extract terms (missing items are listed, never guessed), compare offers, estimate take-home pay, and draft negotiation emails.

## 7. Privacy
- Settings shows exactly which integrations are configured. You can disconnect Gmail, export all your data, or delete your account at any time.
